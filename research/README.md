# Condition Dossiers (01–28)

The raw evidence base. Each file is one deep-research dossier on a single real-world Indian room or usage condition, written from the shared prompt in [`../research_prompts_room_conditions.md`](../research_prompts_room_conditions.md).

**Every dossier has the same 12 sections:**
1 Condition definition · 2 Aerodynamic physics · 3 Role of speed / RPM · 4 Blade & fan geometry levers · 5 Materials & manufacturing · 6 Current market solutions · 7 Academic & industry research · 8 Patent landscape · 9 Unsolved gaps · 10 Measurement & validation plan · 11 Sensors & smart control · 12 Summary, then Sources.

So `R22 §8` in the synthesis docs means *the patent landscape section of dossier 22*.

**Score** = rubric total out of 40 ([`../synthesis/rubric.md`](../synthesis/rubric.md)). **Tier** 1 ≥ 30, 2 = 24–29, 3 < 24. ★ = current focus.

## Caveats
- Claims tagged `[UNVERIFIED]` could not be confirmed from a primary source. Check them before using them in the pitch.
- Patent numbers and assignees are directional. They are **not** a freedom-to-operate opinion.
- Prevalence (how common a condition is) is qualitative in almost every dossier.
